03 / What It Does

The feature set.

The point isn't that it replies fast. It's that it replies right. It knows a brand-new stranger from someone you already know from a personal friend, and treats each one the way you would.

Every comment, answered

It's there for every person who reaches out, day or night, so a warm conversation never goes cold while you're away.

It knows who's who

A stranger, a contact you know, and a personal friend are three different conversations. SMRT tells them apart and treats each one right.

It's honest about what it is

It opens by telling people it's your AI assistant, warm and in one line, then gets straight to helping. No pretending it's you, no awkward reveal three messages in. Honest from hello, still in your voice.

It knows when to step back

The moment you join a conversation yourself, it goes quiet and lets you lead. You never look automated.

It never guesses

Anything it genuinely can't read, a shared reel, a link, it hands straight to you instead of replying into the void.

It protects your name

It respects how each person wants to hear from you, on every channel. Your relationships stay yours.

It answers every time

Comment the keyword today, and again next month... it responds with the whole story in view. Already on the list? It says so, warmly. It never re-asks for what you’ve already given, and it never goes silent.

It never overpromises

The DM goes out first... the public reply follows it. So when it says a message is on the way, one already is. No empty promises under your posts.

It follows up, once

If someone asks for the update and drifts off before sharing their email, it sends one gentle nudge while the conversation is still warm... then it lets it rest.
